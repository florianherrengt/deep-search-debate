Come up with original product concepts built around this core idea:

Companies pay to put their product, logo, or brand in a **highly visible premium spot on a public website**, but those spots are **scarce and competitive**. A company cannot simply buy a permanent ad placement; other companies can challenge, take over, displace, or otherwise compete for the same valuable position.

The appeal should be similar to the Million Dollar Homepage: being part of the site is itself valuable because the site becomes a public spectacle people want to visit and talk about. But here, the most desirable placements are **contested**, creating ongoing competition between brands.

The competition should make the site more interesting to spectators and give participating companies a reason to share it, defend their position, challenge competitors, or draw attention to what is happening.

The exact form of the premium spots and the competition mechanic is what you need to invent. Do not anchor on maps, territories, towers, pixels, rankings, or any other specific implementation.

The ideas should be:

- immediately understandable
- visually compelling
- based around scarce premium placement
- naturally competitive
- capable of generating public drama or spectacle
- attractive enough that companies would actually pay for the exposure
- simple enough to build as a web product
- potentially viral

Most importantly, the core concept must not already exist in substantially the same form. Reject ideas that are just cosmetic variations of an existing product.

Find the strongest original ways to turn **companies competing and paying for control of premium public visibility** into a product.
